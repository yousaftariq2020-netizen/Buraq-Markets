Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Buraq Markets ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website and client portal. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide during registration, such as name, email address, and contact information.
  • Identity verification (KYC) documents, including government-issued ID and a selfie photo, submitted for compliance purposes.
  • Deposit and withdrawal records, including payment references and uploaded payment proof/slip images.
  • Trading account activity, balances, and transaction history within your client dashboard.
  • Technical information such as login timestamps, used to keep your account secure.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your trading account.
  • To verify your identity in line with standard KYC/AML compliance practices.
  • To process deposit and withdrawal requests and confirm payment proof.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account, transactions, and support requests.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our platform.

4. Storage & Security

Your data, including KYC documents and deposit proof images, is stored securely using access-controlled cloud infrastructure. Uploaded documents are kept in private storage and are only accessible to you and authorized Buraq Markets administrators for verification purposes — they are never made public.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared where required by law, regulation, or to process your payments through relevant financial channels.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting our support team, subject to our regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via our Contact page.
